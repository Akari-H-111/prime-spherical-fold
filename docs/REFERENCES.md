# References and admission boundaries

## External arithmetic inputs

1. K. Matomaki, *The distribution of alpha p modulo one* (2009),
   DOI `10.1017/S030500410900245X`, Theorem 1, printed p.267:
   [author-hosted PDF](https://users.utu.fi/ksmato/wp-content/uploads/sites/1432/2025/04/alphapmod1.pdf).
   The homogeneous theorem supplies infinitely many prime approximations with
   exponent `1/3 - epsilon`. The squared sine estimate is a corollary, not a
   new prime-approximation exponent. No inhomogeneous `1/3` claim is made.
2. Champagne--Le--Liu--Wooley, *Well-distribution modulo one and the primes*:
   [author-hosted PDF](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~twooley/publ/20240627welldist.pdf).
   The introduction recalls Vinogradov's equidistribution of `alpha*p` for
   irrational `alpha`. Only prefix equidistribution is used; the stronger
   well-distribution property is not asserted for the frequency in this work.
3. The all-integer control follows from the geometric-sum argument and Weyl's
   criterion, written directly in the arithmetic results.

The consulted PDF hashes are retained in `evidence/PROVENANCE.json`; no PDFs
or third-party manuscript excerpts are redistributed.

## Research provenance and independence

The scalar filling terminology was motivated by the author's HMR Information
Topology work. This repository defines the actual one-dimensional model and
proves its interface directly. No unpublished external theorem is needed for
the scalar identity or Lean development. The original geometry groundwork
was developed in HMR and continued locally into the complete A--E proof.

Related geometry work motivated separating source regularity, image double
points, and observation folds. No Stokes, cross-cap, or operator theorem is
transported merely by analogy. The additive phase `exp(2*i*p)` supplies no
bridge to RH/BigBridge's multiplicative prime frequencies or Weil positivity.
Prime Dimensionality Reconstruction is independent and is not a dependency.
Private research boards, source maps, manuscripts, and historical Git history
are outside this public export.
